Privacy policy

Status Ledger: Time in Status for Jira. Last updated 2026-11-10.

Summary

Status Ledger does not collect, store, sell or share personal data. It has no server of its own. It runs on Atlassian Forge, inside your Jira Cloud site, and reads Jira data only in the browser of the person using it, with that person's permissions.

What the app reads

When you open the report or the issue panel, the app asks Jira, through the Forge bridge, for: issue key, summary, type, assignee display name, current status, created date and the status changes in each issue's history. The permission requested is read:jira-work. The app cannot change anything in Jira.

What the app stores

Nothing on any server. Your working-schedule settings (working days, hours, time zone, holidays, last query) are kept in your browser's local storage, in the app's frame, and never leave your device. Clearing the browser's site data removes them.

What leaves your Jira site

Nothing. The app makes no network calls outside Atlassian (no analytics, no tracking, no third-party services). The CSV export is created in your browser and saved only where you choose.

Atlassian

Atlassian hosts the app and its licensing as part of Forge and the Marketplace. Atlassian's own processing is described in the Atlassian Privacy Policy.
